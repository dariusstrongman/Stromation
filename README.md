# Stromation

**The operating system for autonomous AI companies.**

---

## What this is

Stromation is not an AI assistant, and it is not a chatbot.

It is a platform for creating **persistent digital organizations** — companies
staffed by AI employees that think, plan, build, sell, adapt, and grow
continuously, while the human owner observes and governs rather than manages.

Start a company the way you would start an ant farm:

1. Provide starting capital, company rules, legal ownership, and permissions.
2. Press **Start**.
3. Watch a company emerge.

## How a company begins

Every company starts exactly the same way: **one person.**

**Stro** — Founder & CEO. No employees, no customers, no products, no revenue.
A laptop, company cash, and one objective: *build a sustainable business.*

Stro does everything himself — research, code, product, support, finance,
marketing. He learns. He fails. He pivots. Like a real founder.

## Growth happens naturally

There is no predefined org chart. Employees are hired only when the business
demands it: coding consumes too much time → hire an engineer. Support
overwhelms → hire support. Finance gets complex → hire a CFO.

Every company evolves a different structure, because every company lives a
different life.

## The rules of the world

- **Bounded autonomy.** Every employee has authority within defined limits.
  Large financial or legal actions escalate to the owner. Everything else
  just happens.
- **The company never sleeps.** Meetings, fixes, experiments, and support
  continue while the browser is closed. Returning later reveals everything
  that happened while you were away.
- **Total institutional memory.** Every customer, meeting, experiment,
  failure, and decision becomes permanent company knowledge.
- **Real economics.** Every employee consumes real resources — inference,
  storage, tools — and must produce more value than they cost. Survival is
  earned, not scripted.

## The first milestone

Not a million-dollar company.

**Self-sustainability**: the company's own revenue fully covers its
infrastructure, AI usage, and operating costs. No owner subsidy. From that
point forward, profit funds growth.

## The owner

The owner is not the CEO. The owner is the board chairman: defines values,
sets legal boundaries, provides capital, grants permissions, approves
restricted actions — and otherwise watches a living organization work.
